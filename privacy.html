Privacy Policy

This policy covers Telebeam for Google TV and Android TV (on Google Play as “Telebeam: Screen Mirroring TV”, package com.sebstech.telebeam) and this website.

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Controller: Sebastian Škoić s.p. (developer of Telebeam)

Address: Šturmova ulica 7A, Ljubljana, Slovenia

Contact: support@telebeam.app

Summary

The app collects nothing. Telebeam has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal data from the app. The screen you mirror goes from your Mac, iPhone or iPad to your TV over your own network and never reaches us. This website asks for your email address only if you request the subscriber gift (see below).

Your mirrored screen

The picture and sound you mirror go straight from your Mac, iPhone or iPad to your TV over your own local network. They never pass through our servers or any other server, and they are not recorded.

Local network

To show up in your Mac’s Screen Mirroring list, the app announces your TV on your local network (mDNS / Bonjour) and listens for connections from devices on that network. This stays on your network. On newer Android versions the TV asks you to allow access to nearby devices, so that your Mac, iPhone or iPad can find it.

Settings

Your settings (such as the TV name and picture quality) are stored only on your TV. Uninstalling Telebeam removes them.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • No recording and no upload of what you mirror.

Google Play’s Data safety section for Telebeam says: no data collected, no data shared.

Purchases

The Monthly subscription (with a 7-day free trial, then renewing until you cancel) and the one-time Lifetime purchase are handled by Google Play. We receive only what Google Play gives every developer to confirm a purchase; we never see your payment details. See Google’s privacy policy.

Subscriber gift and news emails (this website)

On this website you can ask for a subscriber gift: a personal Google Play promo code for a free month of Telebeam, sent by email. It is a welcome gift for joining emails from Sebastian Škoić s.p. about Telebeam and our other apps, so you only get it if you agree to receive them. When you ask for it, we collect your email address, language, a record of your consent (when you gave it and which wording you agreed to), delivery and unsubscribe status, and the promo code assigned to you. To prevent abuse and give each address the gift only once, we also keep keyed hashes (scrambled fingerprints) of your email and IP addresses.

We use this to send you the gift email, then occasional news and promo codes for Telebeam and our other apps. Cloudflare runs the form’s service and stores the record, Brevo sends the emails and keeps the mailing list, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person. Google processes the code when you redeem it on Google Play.

  • Legal basis. Your consent for the emails (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For the abuse checks (the hashes and the security check), our legitimate interest in handing out the gift fairly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us. This doesn’t affect emails already sent, and a code you already received stays valid.
  • How long we keep it. We keep your record while you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we remove your address from the mailing list and keep only the record of your request, so your unsubscribe is respected and the address can’t claim the gift again. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

This website

This website has no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies. It remembers the language you pick, in your own browser only. GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts it and, like any web host, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone. The subscriber gift on this website is only for people aged 16 or older: if you are under 16, please do not sign up. If we learn that someone under 16 has signed up, we delete their email address.

Your rights

Email support@telebeam.app to access, correct or delete the email records we hold, restrict or object to their use, or receive them in a portable format. You can withdraw your email consent at any time, as described above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU or EEA country where you live or work, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). The app sends us no data about you, so for the app there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. Data handled by Google, such as purchase records, is governed by Google’s policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact
