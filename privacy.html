Screenbridge privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Applies to the Android TV / Google TV app "Screenbridge: Screen Mirroring" (com.sebstech.screenbridge), published by Sebastian Skoic, s.p., Ljubljana, Slovenia.

What we collect

Nothing. Screenbridge has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Your mirrored screen

The picture and sound you mirror go straight from your Mac, iPhone or iPad to your TV over your own local network. They never pass through our servers or any other server, and they are not recorded.

Local network

To show up in your Mac's Screen Mirroring list, the app announces your TV on your local network (mDNS / Bonjour) and listens for connections from devices on that network. This stays on your network.

Settings

Your settings (such as the TV name and picture quality) are stored only on your TV.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive only what Google Play gives every developer to confirm a purchase; we never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support@controlmymac.com.
