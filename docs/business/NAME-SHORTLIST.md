# New app name: shortlist (28 Oct 2026)

Why: "Screenbridge" is already used by a connected-TV ad agency (screenbridge.tv, Filmkraft.tv, since Dec 2024). Sebastian: "find the best SEO name you can." Play package id `com.sebstech.screenbridge` stays (users never see it).

## How search works here (why the title matters more than the name)

- People type "screen mirroring", "screen mirroring tv", "mirror mac to tv", "cast to tv", "airplay receiver". Play matches whole words in the title (weighted most), then the short and full description.
- A joined-up name like "Telemirror" does not match the word "mirror". So the search words go in the title after the name: **"<Name>: Screen Mirroring TV"** (fits 30 characters for an 8–9 letter name). "Mac" and "AirPlay" stay out of the title (Apple trademarks); they're already in the short and full description.
- The name itself should then be **distinctive**: easy to trademark, and anyone who searches it on Play or Google finds only us. Purely descriptive names ("Mirror TV", "TV Mirroring") can't be owned and drown among 100+ "mirror" apps (checked: "mirror…" searches on Play return makeup mirrors and casting apps).
- A name hinting at TV in many languages helps people remember and click it: "Tele" reads as TV in German, French, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Dutch and others; "Beamer" means projector in German (our biggest market).

## Recommendation: Telebeam

It reads as "TV + beam" almost everywhere we sell, the title carries the two biggest search phrases ("screen mirroring", "tv"), nobody in TV, display or software uses the name, and no live trademark is in the way. Email: support@telebeamapp.com (iCloud+ custom domain for receiving, Brevo for sending, the way controlmymac.com works). Close second if he'd rather own the exact .com: Tellybeam (tellybeam.com is free), at the cost of "telly" meaning little outside the UK.

Not a lawyer's clearance: free exact-word searches only (USPTO, TMview); company registers and social handles not checked. A short lawyer check before spending on the brand is still worth it.
