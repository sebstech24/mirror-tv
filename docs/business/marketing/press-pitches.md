# Screenbridge press pitches

*Prepared 28 Oct 2026. Nothing has been sent or drafted in Gmail. Every batch needs Sebastian's yes. Targets and contacts: `press-targets.csv` (same folder).*

## Rules for every pitch (from the Control My Mac outreach)

- Plain text, no images, no attachments. **2 links at most**: the Play tracking link for that outlet (`play_link` column in `press-targets.csv`, `utm_content` = the outlet slug) and `[DEMO VIDEO LINK]`. For outlets that care about code (XDA, Android Police, Mac & i) swap the demo link for https://github.com/sebstech24/mirror-tv .
- Facts only. Never call the app free: it's paid (Monthly with a 7-day trial, or Lifetime). "Open source (GPL-3.0)" is fine. No prices.
- Tested so far only on a **Philips Google TV with macOS**. Say "made for Google TV and Android TV"; never claim Sony, TCL or Hisense work.
- Don't say the app is made, approved or sponsored by Apple or Google. Say "the Mac's own Screen Mirroring" and leave "AirPlay" for the technical line.
- Review offer: a **Lifetime promo code** from the Play Console, sent only after they say yes (`[PROMO CODE]` below). The code never depends on coverage or a rating. Not from the Control My Mac codes ledger.
- Max 150 words from the greeting to the offer question, personal line included (templates are about 115–135 words, so keep `{{first_line}}` under about 20 words). The address-source and opt-out line and the postal line always stay (GDPR Art. 14, UWG in Germany).
- Before the first send, check the menu names on a Mac set to that language (Control Center → Screen Mirroring, listed per language below).

Placeholders: `{{name}}`, `{{first_line}}` (the personal line, right after the greeting), `{{play_link}}`, `{{address_source}}` (where the address was published, e.g. "on your contact page").

**Batch 3: priority 3.** Only if batch 1–2 got replies or coverage; otherwise rethink the angle first.

**Follow-up rule:** at most one nudge, 7 days after the first email, as a reply in the same thread, no links, only if there's no reply. Never nudge anyone who replied, covered it, or is iMagazine. Outlets with an open Control My Mac conversation are NOT held back (Sebastian, 28 Oct).
