## AlternativeTo (alternativeto.net)
- **Name:** Screenbridge
- **Platforms:** Android TV, Google TV (the sender is macOS's built-in Screen Mirroring; also iOS/iPadOS)
- **License:** Open source (GPL-3.0), commercial on Google Play
- **Tagline:** Turn your Google TV or Android TV into a screen mirroring receiver for your Mac.
- **Description:** Screenbridge makes a Google TV or Android TV show up in the Mac's own Screen Mirroring menu, so you can mirror a MacBook or iMac to the TV, or use the TV as a second display, without an Apple TV or any app on the Mac. Picture up to 4K with sound and low delay, a TV-first interface run with the remote, and clear on-screen help when the Mac and TV can't see each other. No ads, no account, no data collected. The source code is public under GPL-3.0; the Play version is the signed, supported build.
- **Tags:** screen-mirroring, airplay-receiver, android-tv, google-tv, mac, open-source, second-screen
- **Suggest as an alternative to:** AirScreen, AirServer, Apple TV (as a mirroring target), AirReceiver, AirPin, Reflector, UxPlay, RPiPlay, AirPlay Receiver (jqssun), AirBeamTV, Mirroring360
