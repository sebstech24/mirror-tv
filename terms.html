1. What the app does

Telebeam turns your Google TV or Android TV into a receiver for the Screen Mirroring built into your Mac (and iPhone and iPad). Your Mac and TV need to be on the same network. Telebeam is an independent app; it is not made, sponsored or approved by Apple.
