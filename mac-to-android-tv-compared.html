When Telebeam fits

Your TV runs Google TV or Android TV

Telebeam installs on the TV, so there’s no box to add. On a Chromecast with Google TV or a Google TV Streamer, any TV it’s plugged into works.

You want the Mac’s own Screen Mirroring

Pick the TV in Control Center: mirror the whole Mac with sound, or use the TV as a second display. iPhone and iPad work too.

Up to 4K

Auto uses 1080p at 60 fps, the smoothest from a Mac. 4K and 120 fps are there too, marked Beta.

Stays on your home network

The picture and sound go straight from your Mac to your TV. No ads, no tracking, and the code is open source (GPL-3.0).

When another way fits better

  • The Mac sits next to the TV. An HDMI cable is reliable and cheap.
  • No shared Wi-Fi. Guest, hotel and many office or school networks keep devices from seeing each other; a cable doesn’t need the network.
  • An older Chromecast without a remote. It has no app store, so Telebeam can’t be installed on it.
  • The TV doesn’t run Google TV or Android TV. Telebeam can’t be installed on it; a Chromecast with Google TV or Google TV Streamer plugged into the TV can run it instead.
  • The TV is already in the Mac’s list. Some TVs have a receiver built in. Check Control Center → Screen Mirroring first; then you may not need Telebeam.
  • An Apple TV is already on that TV. It does the job; Telebeam is for TVs without one.

Questions people ask

How do I connect my Mac to an Android TV?

With an HDMI cable if the Mac sits next to the TV. Wirelessly, install Telebeam on the TV and pick it in Control Center → Screen Mirroring on the Mac. Casting from Chrome and an Apple TV are the other ways.

Is an HDMI cable better than wireless?

A cable is reliable and cheap if the Mac sits next to the TV; many Macs need a USB-C to HDMI adapter. With Telebeam, the Mac can stay where you are, as long as Mac and TV are on the same Wi-Fi.

Can I mirror a Mac to a Chromecast?

To a Chromecast with Google TV, yes: Telebeam installs on it, and it appears in the Mac’s Screen Mirroring. An older Chromecast without a remote has no app store, so Telebeam can’t be installed on it.

Is casting from Chrome the same as Screen Mirroring?

No. Chrome’s Cast option sends a tab or your screen from inside Chrome. Telebeam puts the TV in the Mac’s own Screen Mirroring, so you can mirror the whole Mac or use the TV as a second display.
