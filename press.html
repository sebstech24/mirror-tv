About Telebeam

93 words

Telebeam is a Google TV and Android TV app made by solo developer Sebastian Škoić in Slovenia. It makes the TV appear in the Screen Mirroring list that is already built into the Mac, so a MacBook or iMac can mirror to the TV or use it as a second display, with sound and up to 4K. Everything stays on the home network: no account, no ads, no data collected. Telebeam is open source under GPL-3.0, starts with a 7-day free trial on its Monthly plan, and also sells a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Telebeam is an independent app and is not made, sponsored or approved by Apple. Your Mac’s Screen Mirroring is part of macOS and uses Apple’s AirPlay technology; Telebeam is the receiver on the TV side.
