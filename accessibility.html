Accessibility

Made for the remote.

Telebeam runs on your TV and is used with the TV remote. This page says plainly what it supports today, and what we have not tested, so you can decide before you try it.

What works today

Everything with the remote

Big, simple screens you control with the TV remote. No keyboard, mouse or phone needed on the TV side.

Large text on the TV

Telebeam’s screens use large text meant to be read from across the room.

The steps on screen

The TV shows the three steps to mirror your Mac itself, so anyone at home can follow them.

Plain words when something goes wrong

If something goes wrong, the TV tells you in plain words what to do: same Wi-Fi, choose the TV again, or set the Mac to mirror the whole screen.

What we have not tested

We have not tested Telebeam with TalkBack or other accessibility features of Google TV and Android TV, so we do not claim support for them. We will update this page as we test more.

Tell us what gets in your way

A screen that is hard to read, a button the remote can’t reach: we want to know and we read every message.
